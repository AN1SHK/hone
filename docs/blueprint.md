# HONE — Product Blueprint

### A gym for attention

*Working name. A software-only daily system that **measures** your attention with a validated test, **shifts** you into a focused state, and **trains** the one mental muscle research actually shows improves focus: catching your mind wander and pulling it back.*

---

## First principles

- **You can't train what you don't measure.** Every session starts with an objective weigh-in.
- **Focus is two things, not one.** *State* = get focused right now. *Trait* = get more focusable over weeks. The product trains both, separately.
- **The trainable core is meta-awareness** — noticing your attention drifted and returning it. That single act is the rep. It's the isolable active ingredient inside meditation.
- **Blocking and soothing don't change the underlying faculty.** They remove the trigger or calm the nerves. Training changes the person.
- **Honesty about transfer is the moat.** This category was burned by overclaiming (Lumosity's $2M FTC settlement). Being the one product that proves — or admits — its transfer wins trust.

---

## The gap (why this can win)

| Category | Examples | What it does | What it *doesn't* do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blockers | Freedom, Cold Turkey, Opal, Forest | Remove distractions | Never trains or measures the mind |
| Soothers | Brain.fm, Endel, Headspace | Induce a transient state via audio | No training loop, no metric |
| Coworking | Focusmate, Flow Club | Behavioral accountability | No cognitive training or measurement |
| Brain-games | Lumosity, Peak, Elevate | Train generic cognition | Weak far-transfer; reputational damage |
| EEG wearables | Muse, Neurable, Mendi | Measure brain activity | $250–1,500 hardware; opaque metrics |
| "Attention gyms" | Attenza, Focuse, NeuroTracker | Train + score | Single-paradigm; non-validated in-house tests |

**No one combines all four:** validated objective measurement + honest progressive training + credible transfer stance + software-only, no hardware. That's the wedge.

## What the science supports (build on these)

| Mechanism | Evidence | Effective dose |
|---|---|---|
| Focused-attention meditation | **Strong** — most replicated attention intervention | 12–20 min/day, 5×/wk, 4+ weeks |
| Meta-awareness (drift-detect → return) | **Moderate** — the active ingredient inside the above | Embedded in the FA sessions |
| Implementation intentions (if-then plans) | **Strong** — Gollwitzer meta, d = 0.65 | One line at block start |
| Attention-residue control (clean breaks) | **Strong** — Leroy 2009 | 30–60s closure before switching |
| Progressive blocks + goal-reactivation breaks | **Moderate** — Ariga & Lleras 2011 | Brief breaks reset the vigilance decrement |
| Sleep + acute exercise | **Strong moderators** — sleep loss wrecks vigilance most | Readiness levers, not the core |
| Arousal-matching to the "middle" (breathing / movement) | **Strong mechanism** — Yerkes-Dodson / LC-NE | 2–3 min pre-work, matched to state |

The organizing model is the **inverted-U of arousal**: the product's job each session is to move the user to the *middle* — down-regulate the anxious, up-regulate the groggy.

## What to avoid (credibility)

Dual n-back "far transfer" (near-transfer only); generic brain-game suites (Lumosity/FTC precedent); binaural beats and 40 Hz for cognition (weak/null acutely); the "90-minute biological clock" (unsupported for waking cognition); Kotler-style "flow hacking" neurochemical-cocktail claims; and "transient hypofrontality" as a literal prefrontal shutdown (fMRI shows *selective* down-regulation, not global).

---

## Measurement layer

- **Daily state gauge — PVT-B** (3-min reaction/vigilance tap test). Near-zero practice effect (its defining strength for repeat testing), extremely sensitive to sleep/fatigue. Metrics: mean 1/RT and lapses. Report as *within-person trend*, not a clinical absolute.
- **Weekly trait gauge — SART** (mind-wandering / response inhibition) or a short **MOT** (tracking capacity, best-in-class test-retest, and naturally gamifiable).
- **The honesty layer:** adaptive difficulty on the *trained* task, plus a *separate untrained probe* to demonstrate — or honestly report — transfer. This is the anti-Lumosity.

---

## The daily protocol (the product loop)

1. **Weigh-in** (2–3 min) — PVT-B reaction test → a "Sharpness" score.
2. **Prime / flow-entry** (~4 min) — set intention (if-then + first action) → clear attention residue → arousal-match breathing or movement → visual anchor.
3. **Train the muscle** (5–20 min, progressive) — focused-attention block; tap every time you *catch* your mind wandering. Catches are reps, not failures.
4. **Deep-work block** (25 / 50 / 90 min) — intention pinned, distractions parked (not acted on), reactivation micro-break, post-block self-rating.
5. **Restore** — restorative break (walk / NSDR), explicitly steered away from social media (which reloads residue).

---

## Metrics that matter (to the user)

Sharpness (reaction), catches-per-minute trend (meta-awareness improving = fewer, faster catches over time), deep-work minutes, streak, self-rated focus.

## Business (brief)

Freemium: free measurement + basic training; paid = adaptive programs, full history, deeper protocols, weekly trait tests. Undercuts the $250–1,500 EEG tier. The brand is *honest transfer* in a category that lied.

---

## v1 scope (the prototype)

Reaction/vigilance test, prime sequence with animated breathing pacer, meta-awareness trainer, deep-work timer with distraction parking, progress charts, and an honest science panel. Local persistence so progress accrues.
