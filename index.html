  • Leave the work room — you must step out of where you work, every break
  • Get into daylight — sunlight resets alertness and steadies your body clock
  • Walk, no phone — clears the mental fatigue that builds up under focus
  • Stretch or move — a few minutes of stretching or light exercise loosens tight muscles and lifts your energy
  • Look far away — a long, soft gaze relaxes the eyes and the mind with them
