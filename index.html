Restore — don't skip this

The break isn't idle time — it's when the last block sinks in and your attention refuels. Skip it and the next block starts foggy.

Do
  • Get into daylight — sunlight resets alertness and steadies your body clock
  • Walk or move — clears the mental fatigue that builds up under focus
  • Look far away — a long, soft gaze relaxes the eyes and the mind with them
  • A few slow breaths — long exhales bring you down from work tension
  • 10–20 min NSDR / yoga-nidra — the deepest reset; refills dopamine and clears the last task from your head
  • Water, and a real snack if hungry — steady fuel beats a sugar spike and crash
Avoid
  • Feeds, social, news — fast novelty spikes dopamine and leaves the next block flat
  • Email and messages — each one opens a loop your mind keeps chewing on
  • Starting a new task — its leftover "residue" bleeds straight into your deep work
  • Bright screens up close — the opposite of the far, calm gaze your eyes need
